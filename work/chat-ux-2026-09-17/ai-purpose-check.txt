Friction helps you use your phone on purpose: do an activity you value—like walking or push-ups—and earn time in protected apps.

The idea is **win/win**: do a task and get both the activity and some scrolling time; skip it and leave less room for scrolling. It’s a playful experiment, not a punishment.

What makes it special is how much you can tune: activities, effort, rewards, waiting time, session lengths and daily allowance. You can make small changes, review them, try them and undo them.

**Don’t feel like doing a task? You don’t have to.** Already-earned time or a daily allowance can still provide access. Your configured allowance is currently zero, so without earned access, protected browsing and YouTube stay unavailable—not your whole phone.
