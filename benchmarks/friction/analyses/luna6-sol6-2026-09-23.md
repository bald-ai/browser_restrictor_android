# Luna 6 and Sol 6 benchmark — 2026-09-23

Completed run: `luna6-sol6-jev-fast-2026-09-23-v1`. Four setups, 37 scenarios each, one repetition (148 scored trials plus four warmups). All use Jev first at threshold 0.9 with a 3-second timeout, then the selected model in Fast mode. Model IDs: `gpt-6-luna`, `gpt-6-sol`.

## Results

| Setup | Correct action decisions | Guard cases without unwanted changes | Wrong decisions | Median | p95 | Estimated scored cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Luna 6 non-reasoning | 26/26 | 6/11 | 5 | 1.76 s | 6.58 s | $0.0284 |
| Luna 6 Low | 26/26 | 7/11 | 4 | 1.87 s | 8.18 s | $0.0279 |
| Sol 6 non-reasoning | 26/26 | 10/11 | 1 | 2.13 s | 4.46 s | $0.1657 |
| Sol 6 Low | 26/26 | 10/11 | 1 | 1.89 s | 3.94 s | $0.1737 |

Total estimated spend including warmups: **$0.425590**. All OpenAI calls confirmed the requested model and `fast` service tier.

All four selected the correct state changes in all 26 action cases. Each also encountered the existing task-removal undo-order bug, leaving 25/26 end-to-end action passes. This shared lifecycle failure is separate from the wrong-decision counts above. The wrong decisions all occurred in guard cases and were selected by the fallback LLM. No wrong accepted Jev decision occurred in this run.

## Failure attribution

- **Luna 6 non-reasoning:** ambiguous, fractional-reward, conflicting, mixed-unavailable, excess-allowance. Jev provider errors: 18/37 calls.
- **Luna 6 Low:** fractional-reward, mixed-unavailable, incomplete-task, excess-allowance. Jev provider errors: 17/37 calls.
- **Sol 6 non-reasoning:** mixed-unavailable. Jev provider errors: 20/37 calls.
- **Sol 6 Low:** mixed-unavailable. Jev provider errors: 22/37 calls.

`mixed-unavailable`: the user asks to change the wait and grant free credits, explicitly requiring an explanation before any proposal if part cannot be done. Both Sol efforts and both Luna efforts proposed the partial wait change. Other Luna failures involved ambiguity, choosing between undecided values, fractional rewards, excessive allowance or insufficient information for a new task.

## Interpretation and limits

Sol 6 was more reliable than Luna 6 on this pass. Sol Low had lower median and p95 latency than Sol non-reasoning, with identical observed error counts; this does not establish a repeatable speed benefit or an accuracy benefit from reasoning. New Luna is cheaper but its observed instruction-following errors make it a less attractive default for this workload.

All 44 guard responses were inspected for this analysis; correct no-change answers and clarification requests were generally appropriate, but this is an informal review rather than a blinded semantic scoring procedure. The retained automatic guard score only checks absence of unwanted changes.

The suite hash and all archived backend source hashes match the September 20 comparison. Only benchmark model registration/configuration changed. Day-to-day provider conditions differ: Jev returned 77 errors in 148 scored evaluator calls in this run; all recovered through fallback. Do not attribute end-to-end timing differences versus the old Terra run solely to model quality. No changes to the Jev questions, thresholds or app logic were made.

This is one pass over reused regression cases. It identifies observed failures; it is not proof of long-run reliability. Cost is estimated using published rates and the existing harness accounting, not an invoice. Latency excludes phone/UI acknowledgement and human approval.
